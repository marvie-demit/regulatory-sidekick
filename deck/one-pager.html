Regulatory Sidekick

A stepwise MDR/IVDR and ISO 13485 implementation — so the team building the device understands and owns its quality system instead of renting it back.

§1

The problem — why first devices stall

The Medical Device Regulation is public. The order of operations is not. A first-time team with no QA/RA hire faces hundreds of interlocking obligations, every guide insisting its own comes first, and “enough” never defined.

The blocking question is never “what does the standard say?” — that text is free. It is “what do I do first?”

§6

Why it is different

  • Not an eQMS. Those sell the container and leave it empty — which makes them partners, not competitors.
  • Not a consultancy. We sell the knowledge once, not the hours of applying it — so the incentive to keep it mysterious never appears.
  • Traceability enforced, not asserted. The build fails if a document has no producing process step. Checked by CI on every deploy.
  • Machine-workable. An admin-approved, audited API lets an AI agent work the same ordered plan — it drafts, but never signs off.
